Trace

A completed study's recorded MCU-load timeline — post-hoc, because outpost capture has no live feed

Nothing here is live. An outpost trace is recorded for the duration of a study and drawn afterwards (embarch-outpost/design.md §3 decision 10) — the one view in this UI that is not push-driven, and the honest shape for the data rather than an omission.

Timeline
ran no closing record, or inside one frame — extent only overlaps a gap — continuity not established records lost marker italic, dotted = no name in the manifest; the number is the answer
Load repartition

Where the CPU's time went, per traced subject. Read the coverage line first: a repartition computed across an interval where records were dropped is not a measurement, and this capture's own losses are stated rather than smoothed over.

SubjectKindEntriesMeasured spansTotalShare of windowNot counted

Four kinds of span are left out of a total and counted in Not counted instead, because their drawn width is an extent and not a duration: one that crosses a gap, one with no closing record, one with no opening record, and one that began and ended inside a single frame — a frame is this capture's resolution, so a span inside one has no measurable length at all. Entries counts every span regardless — “this subject ran N times” survives all four doubts. Shares are of the whole window and deliberately do not sum to 100%: an ISR runs inside whatever it interrupted, and Zephyr's idle thread is reported both as a thread and as idle records.

Gaps — what was lost, and where
FromToRecords lostRow

A gap band is a bound, not a measurement. A gap record is always the first record of the frame that reports it, so the losses happened somewhere between the previous frame's arrival and that one's — From and To are those two arrivals. A gap band is not an empty interval either: the records at both its edges survived and are still drawn.
